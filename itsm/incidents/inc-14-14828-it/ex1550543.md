Users may be unable to connect to their mailboxes in Exchange Online through multiple connection methods

Issue ID: EX1550543
Affected services: Exchange Online
Status: Service degradation
Issue type: Incident
Start time: Nov 12, 2026, 5:17 PM GMT+1

User impact
Users may be unable to connect to their mailboxes in Exchange Online through multiple connection methods. 

More info
Potentially impacted connection methods may include:
- Outlook on the web
- Messaging API (MAPI)
- Exchange ActiveSync (EAS)
- Representational State Transfer (REST) API

Additionally, on-prem mail flow may be affected if it goes to or from Exchange Online. 
 
Impact to additional Microsoft 365 services is described under MO1550821 in the Service Health Dashboard.
